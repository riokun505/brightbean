Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

BrightBean Studio ("we", "our", or "the Service") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle and protect information when you use our video scheduling application.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect third-party platforms to BrightBean Studio, we access only the information necessary to provide the scheduling service:

  • OAuth Access Tokens: Secure tokens provided by platforms (YouTube, TikTok) required to perform video uploads on your behalf.
  • Basic Account Information: Your public profile username, display name, and avatar to identify connected channels in your dashboard.
  • Uploaded Media: Video files, descriptions, and titles that you deliberately select to schedule and publish.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity with TikTok and YouTube APIs.
  • Upload and publish videos you have scheduled to your authorized accounts.
  • Display scheduled post status in your calendar.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or uploaded content to any third parties or advertisers.

3. Storage and Security

All OAuth tokens and credentials stored in BrightBean Studio are encrypted using AES encryption. Tokens are stored locally within your secure database environment.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke access and delete all stored tokens at any time by clicking "Disconnect" next to the account in the Social Accounts settings. Upon disconnection, all tokens associated with your account are immediately and permanently erased from the local database.

5. Third-Party API Policies

BrightBean Studio complies with the API terms of service for connected platforms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, please contact us at privacy@brightbean.studio.
